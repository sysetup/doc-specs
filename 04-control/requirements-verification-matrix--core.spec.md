# Requirements verification matrix specification

## Identity and selection

- **Specification ID:** `REQUIREMENTS-VERIFICATION-MATRIX@core`.
- **Purpose:** Account for planned methods, assessable criteria, activities, evidence, and current verification coverage of controlled technical requirements.
- **Intended readers:** Verification leads and performers, requirement owners, technical reviewers, and authorities reviewing unresolved conformance work.
- **Decision or action supported:** Identify how each applicable requirement will be checked, what has actually been assessed, and which facets or configurations remain unverified.
- **Use when:** A bounded requirement set needs a requirement-by-requirement verification planning and status view, including acceptance-test or preflight classification when the project uses those distinctions.
- **Scope boundaries:** Include controlled technical requirements, their assessable facets, verification mappings, and coverage. A business or mission need, business obligation, or stakeholder requirement is not a technical verification row. Displayed requirement text is a read-only snapshot; results require actual assessment entries, and coverage does not grant product acceptance.

## Authoring inputs and unresolved facts

Inspect the applicable technical requirement sources, IDs, exact locators and editions, current baseline or effectivity, requirement wording and separately assessable facets, target item and configuration rules, established success criteria, method capability, planned activities, responsible performers, environments, and expected evidence. Inspect actual procedures, execution records, deviations, review findings, and acceptance decisions only when reporting that they exist. Determine whether the project classifies particular checks as acceptance or preflight work; a label alone does not make either classification applicable.

If a requirement source, criterion, method, configuration, activity, responsible organization, or evidence is missing, mark the affected facet as a gap or pending decision with its consequence, resolving action, and actual owner if assigned. Return an ambiguous or unverifiable requirement to the project role authorized to resolve its wording; do not invent a threshold. Do not infer a pass from a scheduled event, method selection, evidence ID, or approved matrix. If no applicable requirements were established, state the assessed boundary and source gap rather than creating a sample row or claiming complete verification.

## Finished-document contract

- **Title:** Identify the item or requirement baseline and name the document as its requirements verification matrix.
- **Frontmatter:** None. Begin with the GFM title. Verification basis and state belong in the body.
- **Client or receiving party:** Conditional body element in the identity or scope section. Include it only when a named external party commissions or receives the work; then state that party's established name and relationship, such as client or receiving organization. When no external party exists, state that the document has no external client. Do not invent a party. Do not put the party in the specification ID, filename, or type name.
- **Order:** Identify the controlled requirement set and status rules before the mappings; place coverage and unresolved findings after them. Heading wording may vary.

| Section role | Status and inclusion condition | Content obligation |
|---|---|---|
| Scope, sources, and assessment conventions | Required | Identify the item and configuration boundary, requirement sources and editions, applicable baseline or as-of point, included and excluded obligations, method and phase vocabulary, established project success criteria and the role authorized to resolve them, status and result meanings, and who maintains the matrix. State how any displayed requirement text is refreshed from its source. |
| Requirement verification mappings | Required | Include every in-scope technical requirement or an explicit assessed-empty/source-gap statement. For each separately assessable facet, show the source ID and locator, criterion, selected or pending method, target configuration or sample, planned level/event and responsible role, and expected evidence. Show actual evidence and assessment results only for work that occurred, with the edition and configuration assessed. A selected activity that was not attempted may be recorded as `not-run` with its bounded event and reason, but has no actual evidence. Mark acceptance-test classification when used; include preflight classification only for applicable flight work. |
| Coverage, limits, and unresolved work | Required | Assess each requirement against the union of its facets and real results. Identify unplanned, partial, failed, inconclusive, deferred, or stale coverage, method and environment limits, deviations, open actions, and the route for reassessment after source or item change. State coverage for the assessed configuration without inferring an acceptance decision. |

## Content definitions and forms

| Element | Meaning and cardinality | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Requirement identity | One entry for each in-scope applicable technical requirement; zero only with an explicit assessed-empty or unresolved-source statement. | Give its stable ID, authoritative document and edition, exact paragraph or equivalent locator, and effectivity. A displayed statement MAY be a source-derived read-only snapshot tied to that edition; it MUST NOT become an independently editable master. |
| Assessment mapping | One or more mappings for each assessable facet when a plan exists; zero for a visible uncovered facet. | Several methods or events may jointly cover one requirement. Identify the facet, method, criterion, target item/configuration, planned level or phase, performer, and expected evidence without double-counting the requirement. |
| Success criterion | At least one established comparison rule per planned facet. | Give observable, limit or expected condition, units, tolerance, environment, sampling, and uncertainty treatment where they affect judgment. The criterion must be consistent with the source obligation; a missing value is a gap, not an invented pass/fail rule. |
| Method and activity | A justified inspection, analysis, test, demonstration, or project-authorized alternative for each planned mapping. | Describe the method's assessment steps, comparison rule, required conditions, and material limitations. Identify its controlled project record and edition when one exists. Give an event or procedure reference if established, plus facility, lab, model, or environment when material. Define the project's phase numbering when used. |
| Acceptance and preflight classification | Conditional on the project's actual verification and delivery context. | If acceptance testing is distinguished, say whether the mapped check is part of that program and for which unit or build. For flight work, state preflight applicability; for other work omit that column with a scope reason. These flags do not record an acceptance decision. |
| Evidence and result | Zero or more actual assessment entries per mapping, plus selected unattempted activity entries when relevant; a current summary is optional. | Use `pass`, `fail`, or `inconclusive` only for an attempted assessment, with actual configuration, source/procedure edition, observation locator or missing-observation reason, and review state. `pass` needs an established criterion and supporting observation; `fail` needs an observed miss; an attempted comparison without a usable basis is `inconclusive`. Here `not-run` means a verification activity selected for a bounded event received no assessment action or result assertion; identify that event and reason, without claiming actual evidence or assessed configuration. Identify selected cases and individual attempts when citing a test run. Mere planning has no result. Preserve failures and separate retests; any current summary must explain which applicable entries it represents. |
| Requirement coverage | One assessed state per in-scope requirement: `gap`, `planned`, `partial`, `not-verified`, or `verified`, with the scoped configuration and date/as-of point. | `gap` means a required facet lacks an adequate method or criterion and no assessment was attempted. `planned` means every facet has a suitable method and criterion but none was assessed, including when a selected activity was `not-run`. `partial` means at least one facet has checked passing evidence and at least one lacks it; expose any failure, inconclusive result, or gap. `not-verified` means an assessment occurred but no facet has current checked passing evidence; give the specific failed, inconclusive, invalid, unreviewed, or stale reason and any planning gap. `verified` requires checked passing evidence for every applicable facet at the stated configuration with no unresolved contradictory result. An acceptance or certification decision, when cited, must identify the actual decision record and remains outside the coverage-state vocabulary. |

Use a requirement-centered table or linked tables that expose source ID/locator, facet, criterion, method, configuration, event, performer, expected evidence, actual evidence, result, and coverage. Split long rationale or deviations into notes keyed to the row. A source-derived statement column MAY help review where it can be kept synchronized; the source remains authoritative. Do not reproduce a blank example matrix, impose flight fields on nonflight work, or hide uncovered requirements because they have no event row.

## Quality criteria

- Every applicable controlled technical requirement is accounted for at the correct source edition, including those with no method or criterion yet.
- Method, criterion, environment, sample, and configuration can actually expose the stated obligation; an ID link alone is not coverage.
- Planned work, performed work, reviewed conformance, and any cited acceptance or certification decision have distinct fields and supporting records.
- Failed, inconclusive, invalid, and superseded results remain visible. A later planned retest changes no coverage state; a later checked pass can support `verified` only after any earlier contradictory finding is resolved for the stated scope.
- Counts or completion claims use distinct requirements and declared facets, not duplicate method rows.
