# Technical lookup reference specification

## Identity and selection

- **Specification ID:** `TECHNICAL-REFERENCE@core`.
- **Purpose:** Provide version-bound lookup information for technical interfaces, symbols, commands, or settings.
- **Intended readers:** Developers, integrators, operators, and consumers looking up precise technical behavior.
- **Decision or action supported:** Find an item's syntax, constraints, behavior, errors, and applicability without reconstructing a task narrative.
- **Use when:** A product or component needs an indexed API, SDK, CLI, symbol, schema, or configuration reference.
- **Scope boundaries:** Describe inspectable technical items for lookup; exclude interface agreement authority, tutorials, task procedures, and native-schema validity claims unsupported by actual checks.

## Authoring inputs and unresolved facts

Inspect product and applicable versions, reader lookup tasks, declared subject kinds, exact controlling implementation or native source snapshots, source-derived item inventory and taxonomy, item/version locators, material signatures, types, defaults, errors, effects, lifecycle and applicable subject-specific behavior. Inspect exposed protocol concerns, examples, selected schema or generation inputs and actual checks when used, and drift or maintenance arrangements.

Expose unknown version, inventory, source authority, subject-kind applicability or material attribute with the missing information, affected item and decision, consequence, resolving action and actual owner only when assigned. Distinguish absent and inapplicable from unknown; unknown defaults are not empty, zero or disabled. Missing controlling sources or unresolved material behavior prevent presenting affected definitions as implementation-current or supporting dependent invocation or configuration. An unavailable inventory does not establish completeness or an empty scope. Do not invent items, source values, examples, owners or validation.

## Finished-document contract

- **Title:** Identify the product, reference subject, and covered version or range.
- **Frontmatter:** None. Begin with the standalone GFM title. Put identity, scope, sources and state in the body. Governing source: Type-owned TECHNICAL-REFERENCE@core policy; no external publication metadata schema is selected.
- **Client or receiving party:** Conditional body element in the identity or scope section. Include it only when a named external party commissions or receives the work; then state that party's established name and relationship, such as client or receiving organization. When no external party exists, state that the document has no external client. Do not invent a party. Do not put the party in the specification ID, filename, or type name.
- **Order:** State identity, applicability and source authority before the inventory and lookup index; put common and applicable subject-specific definitions before examples, lifecycle and consistency results.
- **Presentation:** Required roles may share item blocks, tables, paragraphs or sections when their facts, conditions, source locators and gaps remain retrievable. Group by actual subject taxonomy; a compact reference needs no separate administrative chapter per role. This specification contains every selected obligation and requires no supporting override.

## Content definitions and forms

| Element | Meaning and cardinality | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Reference subject | Exactly one coherent product and version-bound subject set in Identity, version, and authority. | Identify included kinds, source editions, controlling artifacts and excluded versions; an unstated version is not current. |
| Coverage reconciliation | Exactly one account in Inventory and item index, repeated by kind or edition when needed. | Compare source inventory, index and definitions in both directions; expose missing, duplicate and orphaned items. An assessed-empty result requires a basis; an unavailable inventory remains a gap. |
| Lookup item | One per declared in-scope item in Inventory and item index and Common item definition; zero only with assessed-empty scope or an explicit unavailable inventory. | Use stable names and usable item/version locators. Give a definition or a visible gap and consequence; an index alone is not lookup coverage. |
| Item attribute | One account for each material common or subject-specific attribute of an item. | Sourced, absent, unknown and inapplicable are distinct dispositions with basis; preserve native values and explicit shared-rule links. |
| Specialized definition | One per item of each included subject kind in Callable details, Command-line details, or Configuration and schema details. | Conditions follow the declared scope; an uncertain kind requires a gap and blocks its affected completeness claim rather than silently omitting obligations. |
| Exposed protocol behavior | One account per applicable operation and exposed concern in Protocol behavior; zero for assessed absence. | Bound continuation, retry, duplicate effects, limits and concurrency to actual source and version; unknown material behavior blocks the dependent invocation or favorable claim. |
| Example and check | Zero or more examples and exactly one actual check-status account in Examples, lifecycle, and consistency. | Keep expected output separate from observed outcome and not-run execution; checks identify actual configuration, method, evidence and limitations. |
| Native or generation binding | One per selected native artifact or generation path in Identity, version, and authority and Examples, lifecycle, and consistency; zero when neither is used. | Identify controlling edition and inspected snapshot, generation inputs and actual validator status when applicable. GFM lookup and finite consistency checks do not establish arbitrary schema validity. |
| Lifecycle, retrieval, and drift basis | Exactly one account in Examples, lifecycle, and consistency, with per-kind or per-version results when needed. | Retain actual lookup observations, gaps, deprecation/removal basis, replacement and update routes; changed sources require reassessment, not an upgraded historical check. |

Use indexed item blocks, signatures or attribute tables and concise version-bound examples. Keep definitions near their usable index locators and link shared rules explicitly. Generated references and native schemas retain their exact inputs, source authority and actual check status; GFM is the human lookup view. Interface control retains agreement authority, while guides and tutorials retain ordered tasks; examples do not grant execution permission.

## Quality criteria

- Product, subject kinds, source snapshots and versions bound every claim; item and version locators let the reader distinguish applicable from excluded or unknown behavior.
- Source inventory, index and definitions reconcile in both directions with no hidden missing, duplicate or orphaned item; unavailable and assessed-empty inventories remain distinct, and each item reaches a definition or visible gap.
- Material attributes are sourced or explicitly absent, unknown or inapplicable with consequences; common and subject-specific definitions preserve signatures, types, defaults, errors, effects and lifecycle without guessing.
- Exposed protocol behavior has version-bound continuation, retry, duplicate-effect, limit and concurrency semantics; unexposed concerns are not universal requirements and unknown material behavior stops dependent use.
- CLI and settings lookups expose applicable parsing, exit channels, authorization, precedence, activation, relationships and sensitive treatment; native fields and values preserve their controlling definitions.
- Examples, generated output, native artifacts and schema checks identify actual source, configuration, method, outcome and limits; expected behavior and not-run product execution are not reported as observed validation.
- Recorded per-kind item/version retrieval checks cover ordinary, incomplete and relevant boundary lookup, gaps and stopping; lifecycle and drift claims remain bound to actual editions and prior results are reassessed after source change.
